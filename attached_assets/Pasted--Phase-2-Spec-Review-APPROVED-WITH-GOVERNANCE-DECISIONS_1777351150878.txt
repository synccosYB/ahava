# Final Product Decisions on Open Questions

---

## 1. Retroactive Scheduled Rule Changes

### Decision:

**Non-retroactive**

### Rule:

* Scheduled policy changes apply only to future evaluations after `effectiveAt`
* Existing in-progress shifts retain original policy calculations unless manually corrected

---

## 2. Maximum Emergency Override Duration

### Decision:

**Soft cap: 30 days**

* Warning above 14 days
* Requires explicit admin acknowledgement above 30 days
* No hard backend block unless future compliance rules demand it

---

## 3. Holiday Calendar Bootstrap

### Decision:

**Do NOT auto-seed**

* Start empty
* Provide one-click import for:

  * US Federal Holidays
  * Custom company calendar templates
  * Medical/healthcare operational templates later

---

## 4. Conflict Acknowledgement Persistence

### Decision:

**Fresh conflict validation required every publish/schedule**

* No permanent dismissals
* Prior acknowledgements logged
* Every new activation/schedule must rerun checks

---

## 5. Publish Permissions

### Decision:

**Create dedicated permission**

### Add:

`policies.publish`

### Rule:

* Admin role alone is insufficient
* Publishing/scheduling/rollback/emergency creation requires explicit publish rights

---

## 6. Version Snapshot Trigger

### Decision:

**Snapshot on:**

* Publish
* Schedule
* Activate
* Rollback
* Emergency override activation

### Draft saves:

* No full version snapshot
* Standard draft persistence only

---

## 7. Holiday Calendar Scope Precedence

### Decision:

**Use existing policy hierarchy**
Employee → Department → Location → Division → Global

---

## 8. Blackout Date vs Existing Approved PTO

### Decision:

**Allow blackout creation**

### But:

* Surface impacted approved PTO list
* Require re-review queue
* Do NOT auto-cancel existing approved PTO
